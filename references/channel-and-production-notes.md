# Channel and Production Notes

Use this reference only when the requester names a channel, supplier, or final production requirement. Treat a verified platform or supplier specification as a hard requirement. Treat all other guidance as a working recommendation.

## General Rules

State the source of a required specification and the date it was checked when the review relies on it. Do not claim a channel, print, file-size, crop, safe-area, or export requirement was verified unless the relevant source or specification was provided or inspected during the current task.

Separate the visible review from technical checks. A graphic can be visually ready while export dimensions, file size, link destination, QR-code function, bleed, colour mode, or supplier template still need confirmation.

## Website or GoHighLevel Graphic

Check image-message fit, visible loading risk only when a file size is available, readable mobile rendering from a supplied preview, and adequate space around text. Do not approve a funnel link or mobile breakpoint without testing it.

## Social or Display Ad

Use verified platform requirements for dimensions and safe areas when they are provided or inspected. Otherwise, review visible readability and say that final platform crop or safe-area checks remain open. For video placements, this skill reviews a supplied still or storyboard frame only unless video-specific review is requested.

## Email Header

Review hierarchy, text legibility, and whether the image supports rather than repeats the email subject or surrounding copy. Request the intended template or preview before approving client rendering.

## Print, Signage, Vehicle Wrap, or Yard Sign

Request the printer or supplier specification before approving bleed, trim, resolution, colour mode, or final production. Consider viewing distance and the smallest essential message. A graphic that works at laptop distance may fail on a sign.

## Export and Naming Guidance

Give export or naming guidance only when the requester needs it. Use a simple pattern such as `client-channel-size-purpose-v01.ext`. Treat this as a working convention, not a technical requirement, unless a client or team standard was supplied.
