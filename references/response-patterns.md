# Response Patterns

Use the shortest pattern that answers the request. Do not fill sections that are not relevant.

## Quick Look: Visually Ready

> **Visually ready.** The headline is easy to spot, the photo supports the local-service message, and the button does not compete with the offer.
>
> **Keep:** Retain the current headline, photo, and layout.
>
> **Final check:** Confirm the exported image remains readable on a phone before calling it fully ready.

Use this pattern when the visible asset works but an untested technical or production check remains.

## Quick Look: Bounded Fix

> **Fix.** The offer is clear, but the headline blends into the background image.
>
> **Keep:** Retain the photo and the current offer wording.
>
> **Change:** Place a solid or darker overlay behind the headline so it reads without zooming.
>
> **Done when:** The headline is readable at normal phone size without covering the subject.

## Quick Look: Multiple Material Issues

> **Fix.** The layout is usable, but two material issues need correction before publishing.
>
> **Keep:** Retain the image and the main offer.
>
> **Change:** Increase headline contrast. Replace the displayed phone number with the number in the approved brief.
>
> **Done when:** The headline is readable at normal viewing size and the contact number matches the approved brief.

Use this pattern only when more than one serious problem is present. Do not add a list of minor preferences.

## Focus Review

> **Fix.** This Facebook ad has a strong offer and clear local image, but the reading order makes the customer action easy to miss.
>
> **Preserve:** Keep the jobsite photograph and the "Same-Week Service" offer.
>
> **Priority fix:** Move the action line below the offer and give it stronger contrast than the support copy.
>
> **Optional check:** Confirm the final placement against the current platform-safe-area requirement.
>
> **Done when:** A viewer can identify the service, offer, and next action in one quick scan.

## Pre-Flight Review

Use these headings only when they matter: **Must fix before publishing**, **Improve if time allows**, **Verified production notes**, **Open checks**, and **Ship when**.

Never label a preference as a must-fix issue. Never mark an untested export or supplier setting as verified.

## Revision Review

> **Previous fixes:** Headline contrast and the approved phone number are now correct.
>
> **Verdict:** Ready, subject to final mobile export check.
>
> **No new design changes:** The remaining spacing difference is a preference, not a material problem.

Close resolved issues clearly. Do not reopen a settled preference merely because an alternative is possible.
